## 2. Governing system
Product meaning → emotional experience → narrative → visual responsibilities → object selection → geometric vocabulary → construction grammar → spatial relationships → material/optical relationships → compositional hierarchy → camera/focus and behaviour → collective choreography → critique. Object roles precede choice of props. Design a world around a product rather than a background behind it. Consistency is a system of relationships, not uniform shape, colour, movement or material. Use literal identity (recurring props), geometric identity (same primitives transformed), semantic identity (same function represented in different forms). A scene may preserve local group relationships without maintaining global physical coordinates. Continuity of design language is more strongly supported than one uninterrupted world.

## 11. Six-scale design hierarchy
World: continuous environment, portal, major architecture. System: complete app and design library. Component: navigation, toolbar, message, calendar. Object: tray, pencil, clips, discs, sheets. Construction: rims, bevels, cavities, layers, glyphs. Micro: repeated surface cells, grid crosses, optical highlights. Camera approach reveals NEW information, not just enlargement of flat artwork. Use fine detail that disappears gracefully at distance. Empty space supports information, physical separation, shadows, motion and attention. Distinguish architectural, functional and peripheral scale. Not all objects move, not every object is beautiful, not every surface is complex.
## 12. Rules and caveats
Shared vocabulary: capsule, slab, disc, sphere, cube, ribbon, sheet, wire, recess, glyph, grid, fragment. Grammar: nest, stack, offset, repeat, scale, sweep, bend, embed, illuminate, occlude, reflow, assemble. Stable groups can coexist with asymmetric composition. Control oppositions: rectilinear/curved, rigid/flexible, smooth/textured, opaque/translucent, order/irregularity, technical/expressive. Material-specific optical effects and geometry-specific movement are more important than literal palette. Use these principles as transferable hypotheses, not verified creator intent. The ultimate measure is an independent high-quality result, not the number of documented objects or the subjective 95% score.
