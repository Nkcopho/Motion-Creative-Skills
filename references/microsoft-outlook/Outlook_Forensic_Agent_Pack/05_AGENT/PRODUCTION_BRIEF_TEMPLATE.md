# Production brief template (copy and complete)
- Product / audience / business objective / conversion action:
- Approved brand assets, colour, type, UI, copy, legal constraints:
- Deliverables (duration, aspect, resolution, fps, renderer/platform, formats):
- Desired emotional experience; unwanted emotional experience:
- Narrative premise, opening hook, evidence/proof, closing action:
- Reference evidence (shot IDs, source frames; what to preserve vs replace):
## Shot contract (repeat)
Shot ID | purpose | audience question | duration | framing | focal object | visual responsibility | background stability | geometry | materials | optical interaction | negative space | entry/hold/exit | focus | dependency | legibility | acceptance tests | known unknowns.
## Object contract (repeat)
Object ID | parent | scene IDs | semantic role | construction primitives | proposed world dimensions/units | screen-space target | material | texture scale | shadow type | layer order | independent motion | visible states | reason selected | alternative rejected | evidence/source | uncertainty.
## Motion event (repeat)
Event ID | actor | trigger | precondition | start/end time | start/end state | translation/rotation/scale/deformation/visibility channels | proposed easing | lead/follow | camera relationship | focus | semantic change | settling | test.
## Critique record
Version | reviewer | hypothesis | reference evidence | change | measurable test | pass/fail | regression | open question. Never mark not tested as pass.
